About Me

A Little About Who I Am.

I’m Sunjeta Jahan Snegdha, a Computer Science and Engineering student at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), majoring in Software Engineering.

I enjoy building practical software: web applications, desktop applications and the databases behind them. Through my projects I’ve worked across web development, object-oriented programming, database design and computer graphics.

Alongside development, I do machine learning research. I’ve authored 3 IEEE conference papers on agentic AI adoption, phishing URL detection and stroke risk prediction (first author on agentic AI adoption), with a focus on making models explainable and reliable.

My goal is to grow as a software engineer while contributing to real projects and meaningful research.
